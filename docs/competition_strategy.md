# RAPIS Competition Strategy — Team WINNER

## NISR 2026 positioning

RAPIS is designed for **Track 1: Agricultural Productivity**. The 2026 NISR criteria allocate 20 points each to:

1. Problem Understanding & Relevance
2. Data Use & Methodology
3. Tech Innovation
4. Usability & Design of Tool/Prototype
5. Tangible Impact

Source: NISR 2026 Hackathon Competition — https://statistics.gov.rw/about/hackathon/2026-hackathon-competition

## Core problem

Decision-makers can have agricultural statistics without having a simple way to move from those statistics to district-level evidence, predictive benchmarking and transparent targeting.

## RAPIS response

**NISR evidence → productivity patterns → validated prediction → transparent targeting → decision action → future climate/geospatial expansion**

## What makes the prototype defensible

- Official-source starting point.
- Clear distinction between description, prediction and causality.
- Group-aware validation that holds out complete districts.
- Out-of-fold predictions rather than in-sample performance alone.
- Transparent targeting formula rather than a black-box priority label.
- No fabricated rainfall, soil, satellite or geographic observations.
- Explicit coverage of NISR Track 1's irrigation and soil-conservation focus.
- Clear future modules for post-harvest/storage, value addition and export opportunities.
- Submission package includes GitHub, deployment and documentation readiness.

## 90-second demonstration

**0–15 seconds:** State the problem and show the Decision Center.

**15–35 seconds:** Select a district/crop and show NISR productivity and practice evidence.

**35–55 seconds:** Open Validated ML and explain district-held-out validation.

**55–75 seconds:** Open Targeting Lab and explain the transparent evidence score.

**75–90 seconds:** Show the impact pathway and next-generation climate/geospatial layer.

## Claims to avoid

Do not say:
- irrigation causes yield to increase by X based on this dataset;
- the model will predict future harvests without multi-season validation;
- this is the official government priority ranking;
- climate risk has been measured before climate data are connected.

Use:
- “observed association”;
- “district-held-out predictive benchmark”;
- “evidence-priority score”;
- “candidate intervention area for further assessment.”
