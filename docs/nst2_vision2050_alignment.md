# NST2 and Vision 2050 Alignment

## Why agriculture productivity is a national-priority problem

The NISR 2026 Hackathon explicitly requires Track 1 solutions to address real agricultural challenges and says tangible practical impact for farmers, agricultural planners and policymakers is encouraged.

RAPIS directly supports this by turning agricultural statistics into decision-support information.

## NST2 (2024–2029)

RAPIS aligns with NST2 priorities around:
- increasing agricultural productivity
- strengthening climate resilience
- increasing exports and value addition
- using ICT and innovation to improve service delivery
- supporting evidence-based planning

The NST2 monitoring framework includes agricultural growth and productivity indicators, with NISR Seasonal Agricultural Survey (SAS) identified as a data source for priority food-crop productivity.

## PSTA 5 (2024–2029)

PSTA 5 is the agriculture-sector implementation framework aligned with NST2 and Vision 2050. Its priorities include modernization and climate-resilient production, sustainable land and water management, modern inputs, mechanization, inclusive markets and post-harvest management.

RAPIS therefore focuses on:
- productivity measurement
- irrigation
- fertilizer/input use
- erosion/land management
- climate information
- spatial targeting
- future value-chain and market modules

## Vision 2050

Vision 2050's agriculture direction is toward modern, market-oriented and climate-resilient agriculture, professional farmers and commercialized value chains.

RAPIS contributes on the information/technology side by providing a reusable data infrastructure and decision-support layer that can help identify productivity patterns and guide evidence-based interventions.

## Alignment statement for presentation

"RAPIS operationalizes national agricultural priorities by converting NISR agricultural statistics and complementary environmental data into localized evidence for productivity, climate resilience and intervention planning. It is aligned with NST2's agricultural transformation agenda, PSTA 5's climate-resilient and productive agri-food systems priorities, and Vision 2050's ambition for modern, market-oriented agriculture."
