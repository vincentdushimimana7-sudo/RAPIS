## What to open
The complete upgraded application is in `r_shiny/`.

### Run it
Open R/RStudio at the project root and run:

```r
source("r_shiny/install.R")
shiny::runApp("r_shiny")
```

Or use:

```r
source("r_shiny/run_app.R")
```
